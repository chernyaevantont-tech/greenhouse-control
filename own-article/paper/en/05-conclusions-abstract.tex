\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}

We asked which open-loop criterion should select a data-driven surrogate for
economic model predictive control (MPC). Figure~\ref{fig:graphical-abstract}
summarises the two findings that answer it.

\begin{enumerate}

%% --- Finding 1 ---------------------------------------------------------
%% Source: regen/results/ladder_rerun/ladder_rerun.csv + ladder_rerun2.csv
%% (1440 rows = 20 seeds x 72 configs, zero duplicates).  QUOTED SLICE:
%%   degree == 1 AND denoise == 'none' AND optimizer in {stlsq, ensemble},
%%   n = 40 rows per library.  SCOPE WARNING, verified by recomputation:
%%   pooled over ALL 72 labels the one-step means are raw 2.703 <
%%   physics_no_cross 2.975 < physics 3.454, i.e. the raw library is best on
%%   one-step error too and there is NO reversal.  The reversal exists only in
%%   the degree-1 block (all denoisers) and is sharpest undenoised.  Do not
%%   restate it as a property of the whole ladder.
%% Held-out replication: regen/results/holdout/holdout_holdout.csv, in_sample
%%   == False, n = 40 per library: one-step 1.895 / 1.819 / 1.765 (raw /
%%   physics_no_cross / physics), median rollout 2.722 / 15.421 / 21.568.
\item \textbf{One-step accuracy and multi-step stability rank the candidate
models in opposite orders.} In the first-order, undenoised block of a
72-configuration identification ladder (54 distinct fits, 20 replicates each),
under the two sparse estimators, the library with the best one-step error was
worst on every multi-step criterion: physically motivated
features lowered one-step RMSE from $1.86$ to $1.68$~\textdegree C but raised
the median 24~h rollout RMSE from $2.67$ to $24.27$~\textdegree C and the
divergence rate from $0.000$ to $0.076$. The reversal belongs to that block ---
pooled over all 72 labels the raw library is the better one-step predictor too
--- but it reproduces on a held-out identification year. Where it holds,
one-step error misleads as a criterion for choosing an MPC surrogate
\cite{lambert2020,wei2023,gevers1993,vandenhof1995,yonezawa2025}.

%% --- Finding 2 ---------------------------------------------------------
%% kappa and non-zero counts: ladder_rerun/ladder_rerun*.csv (degree 1, denoise
%% none). Boiler-term survival: priced_main/*.csv (sindy_mpc_conf, 3 of 20 seeds
%% retain xi_uboil). Knock-in median: priced_mech/mechanism_pricedMech*.csv
%% (priced) vs final/mechanism*.csv (default).
\item \textbf{The mechanism is conditioning, not physics.} Vapour-pressure and
screen-attenuated-radiation features are nonlinear transforms of the same
measured states, hence near-collinear with them: the regression condition
number rose from $8.2$ through $24.5$ to $53.4$, and the fits became
\emph{denser} ($20.1 \rightarrow 37.0$ non-zero terms), not sparser
\cite{cortiella2021,balanceguided2026}. The same ill-conditioning deletes the
boiler coefficient in 17 of 20 fits of the frozen physics-informed recipe;
reinstating it is
significant but shifts the median margin by only $+0.21$~EUR\,m$^{-2}$ under
the corrected objective against $+3.05$ under the defective one --- a
single-coefficient ablation, 20 seeds, one season, whose priced mean is nine
times its median.

%% --- Finding 3 ---------------------------------------------------------
%% Four test seasons (2020-2023), priced objective: priced_main/*.csv,
%% priced_dagger/*.csv, n2_tune/tune_rb_n2.csv, final/main.csv.
%% Unseen seasons (2014-2017), DEFAULT objective: n5_years/main_n5.csv.
%% "Thirteen controllers" (same count as Section 3.4) = seven SINDy-MPC variants
%% (raw, raw_ens, conf, dense, lowthr and two on-policy re-identification
%% variants), nn_mpc, ppo, sac, oracle_mpc and the rule-based heuristic in its
%% stock and tuned form, which are distinct controllers with distinct setpoints.
%% Replicates: 20 seeds for all except nn_mpc (10 seeds, n = 40), either form of
%% the heuristic (deterministic, one run per season, n = 4) and oracle_mpc
%% (n = 60; all 20 runs of 2022 dropped by the solver-abort rule).
%% The test against the tuned heuristic is a ONE-SAMPLE signed-rank test against
%% a constant -- see the footnote to Table~\ref{tab:headline}.
%% n5_years/main_n5.csv contains FOUR methods only (raw_ens, conf, dense,
%% rule_based); no RL, no NN-MPC, no planner, no tuned heuristic.
\item \textbf{A raw, physics-free and sparser library gives the best closed-loop
economics.} Of thirteen controllers on four test seasons at 20 replicates --- 10
for the neural-network MPC, one deterministic run per season for either
heuristic, three seasons for the full-model planner --- the raw-library
ensemble surrogate \cite{fasel2022} led with
$+4.32$~EUR\,m$^{-2}$ against $+3.09$ for the best physics-informed variant
(Holm-corrected $p=7.7\times10^{-4}$, family of 13) and
$+2.26$ for the tuned agronomic heuristic ($\Delta=+2.06$~EUR\,m$^{-2}$, better
in 75 of 80 runs, $p=6.9\times10^{-11}$, a one-sample test against a
deterministic constant) \cite{wilcoxon1945,holm1979}. On four
seasons never used at any stage it led in all four
($+6.42\pm0.95$~EUR\,m$^{-2}$, 8 replicates) --- but under the default
objective, against three comparators only and with the heuristic untuned, so
that block is comparable with the main table in ordering, not in level.

%% --- Finding 4 ---------------------------------------------------------
%% Tuning gain: n2_tune/tune_rb_n2.csv (blocks tuned_test / stock_test) and the
%% 16-trial training scores in the same file. Priced-vs-default deltas:
%% priced_main/*.csv and priced_dagger/*.csv vs final/main.csv and n7/main_n7.csv.
%% Recomputed priced - default, matched on (seed, test_year):
%%   dense +2.738, lowthr +2.676, conf_dagger +1.777, dense_dagger +1.733,
%%   nn_mpc +1.565, conf +1.538, raw_ens +0.246, raw +0.214.
%% The range +2.68...+2.74 therefore covers ONLY the two dense physics variants,
%% not the physics-informed set; the full physics-informed range is +1.54...+2.74
%% and the ratio to the raw variants is 6-13x, not a uniform order of magnitude.
%% The two-thirds figure belongs to the objective correction ALONE (gap
%% 4.0718 - 0.4132 = +3.659 default -> 4.3173 - 3.0888 = +1.228 priced, -66 %).
\item \textbf{Two comparison defects had inflated the effect.} The rule-based
reference called tuned was not:
searching its four setpoints under the 16-trial budget the reinforcement-learning agents
received is worth $+3.49$~EUR\,m$^{-2}$. The stage cost also weighted actuators against
the prices its criterion uses; pricing them improved both axes for all eight MPC
controllers, but by $+2.74$ and $+2.68$~EUR\,m$^{-2}$ for the two dense
physics-informed variants (the remaining physics-informed controllers gained
$+1.54$ to $+1.78$) against only $+0.25$ and $+0.21$ for the raw ones. The gap
between the libraries fell from $+3.66$ to
$+1.23$: this second correction alone removed two-thirds of what had looked
like a library effect. The direction survives, the magnitude does not.

%% --- Finding 5 ---------------------------------------------------------
%% Pareto front: priced_main/*.csv + priced_dagger/*.csv + final/main.csv +
%% n2_tune/tune_rb_n2.csv.  Guard: final/tables/guard.csv (raw final/guard.csv).
%% Coefficient perturbation: priced_design/design_pricedDesign*.csv, deduped on
%% (factor, value, seed, test_year, rep); note that this tree is mislabelled --
%% experiments_support.py hard-codes objective="full", so the perturbation
%% sensitivity is a DEFAULT-objective result and must be described as such.
%% Cross-environment check: the paired adapt wave measured on two machines
%% (closed-loop EPI matched in 0 of 180 cells, mean |delta| 1.33 EUR/m2).
%% Front caveats (both in Section 3.4): sindy_mpc_dense and sindy_mpc_lowthr
%% differ by 0.0066 EUR/m2 and 5 violation steps -- two front points, one
%% controller; sindy_mpc_dense_dagger has 7 of 80 runs truncated and its
%% violation lead over dense narrows from 71 to 26 steps over completed runs.
%% Guard, completed seasons only (n = 421 pairs): delta -0.332, p = 0.081, n.s.
\item \textbf{The advantage is neither Pareto-dominant nor unconditional.} The
front over margin and violations holds five controllers, and PPO
\cite{schulman2017} incurs $1330$ violation steps against the raw library's
$4130$, at $3.86$~EUR\,m$^{-2}$ less margin --- though two of those five points
are one physics-informed controller under two thresholds, and a third owes part
of its position to seven truncated runs. The surrogate has no crop state,
so the objective is a proxy and terminal biomass is unpriced; the
out-of-distribution guard is a negative result (paired median $+0.19$ but mean
$-0.69$~EUR\,m$^{-2}$, $+430$ violation steps, 179 of 600 seasons terminated
early, detector AUC $0.66$, and no significant margin difference once
incomplete seasons are excluded); a $\pm15\,\%$
coefficient perturbation already degrades margin significantly, though under
the original objective and through a minority of catastrophic runs; and
everything is simulation-only, one location, one planting date, with
closed-loop margins that did not reproduce across operating systems.

\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Abstract}

Economic model predictive control (MPC) of greenhouse climate needs a process
model, and sparse identification of nonlinear dynamics (SINDy) offers an
interpretable surrogate; which open-loop criterion should select one
for closed-loop economics is rarely tested. We screened 72
candidate configurations on the GreenLight tomato model under ERA5 weather and
compared thirteen controllers over four test seasons at up to 20
replicates each, scored by the simulator's own economic
performance indicator (EPI, seasonal margin). One-step accuracy and multi-step
stability ordered the candidates in opposite directions in the first-order,
undenoised block under sparse
estimators: physically motivated features improved one-step RMSE from 1.86 to
1.68~\textdegree C while degrading median 24~h rollout RMSE from 2.67 to
24.27~\textdegree C and raising the regression condition number from 8.2 to
53.4. The raw, physics-free library---also the sparser one---gave the best
closed-loop economics, $+4.32$~EUR\,m$^{-2}$ against $+3.09$ for the best
physics-informed variant (Holm-corrected $p=7.7\times10^{-4}$) and $+2.26$ for
an agronomic heuristic tuned under the reinforcement-learning agents' own 16-trial budget
($+2.06$~EUR\,m$^{-2}$, better in 75 of 80 runs against this deterministic
reference, $p=6.9\times10^{-11}$); on
four seasons never used at any stage, against three comparators, it led in all
four ($+6.42$~EUR\,m$^{-2}$, default objective). The advantage is not
Pareto-dominant: the front holds
five controllers, and PPO incurs 1330 constraint-violation steps against 4130.
Correcting the stage-cost actuator weights to the prices the criterion uses
removed two-thirds of the apparent library effect. Screen candidate surrogates
by multi-step stability and conditioning, not by one-step fit.

\subsection{Keywords}

greenhouse climate control; economic model predictive control; sparse
identification of nonlinear dynamics; control-oriented model selection;
multi-step prediction error; regression conditioning; reinforcement learning
baselines; Pareto analysis
