What this licence does not cover
--------------------------------
This archive contains no third-party code or data. The scripts import the
GreenLight tomato greenhouse model as packaged in gl_gym 0.3.1, but neither that
package nor the GreenLight model itself is redistributed here; both carry their
own terms.

The weather used by the study is ERA5/ERA5-Land, retrieved through the
Open-Meteo historical archive API. The weather files are not in this archive
either: make_weather.py reconstructs them by calling that API. ERA5 is a
Copernicus Climate Change Service product and the Open-Meteo API has its own
terms of use; both apply to the data you retrieve, not to this deposit.
